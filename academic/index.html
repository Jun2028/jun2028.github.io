ProofConf

Does a model’s confidence tell us whether its proof is correct?

I built a reproducible pipeline to investigate this question: generate Lean proofs with language models, check them with Lean, and compare token-level confidence signals against verification outcomes.

  1. 01GenerateProofs & token log-probabilities
  2. 02VerifyCorrectness checked by Lean
  3. 03AnalyseConfidence against pass / fail

Includes sliding-window confidence metrics and localisation of low-confidence proof segments.
